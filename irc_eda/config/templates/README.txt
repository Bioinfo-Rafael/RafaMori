These templates illustrate supported gas-phase HF and B3LYP ENPART inputs.
They are NOT valid replacements for the project's M06-2X/SMD calculations.
Actual point inputs are generated only after method/solvent validation.
For LIBRARY, config.iqa.libxc must explicitly specify method, family,
EXC_FUNCTIONAL or EX_FUNCTIONAL + EC_FUNCTIONAL, and verified_equivalence_source.
Only LDA/GGA/HYB_GGA families are enabled. No meta-GGA override is accepted.
The official easyEDA template.gjf and fragment.txt are kept in vendor/easyEDA.
Per-system editable fragment templates are generated in outputs/project.
